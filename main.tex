\section{Research Interests}
I build time-series foundation models for cardiology that forecast adverse cardiac events, such as arrhythmias and cardiac arrest, from continuous ECG monitoring before they occur. I work closely with cardiologists at Penn Medicine to make these models accurate, stable, and trustworthy enough for prospective deployment at the bedside.
