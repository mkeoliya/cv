\section{Research Interests}
I aim to enhance the safety and adoption of ML models in healthcare by (1) developing new interpretable models for critical applications like cancer treatment prediction and sepsis detection, (2) enabling multi-modal reasoning over diverse clinical data sources, and (3) ensuring these models are explainable and trusted by clinicians in high-stakes decisions.
